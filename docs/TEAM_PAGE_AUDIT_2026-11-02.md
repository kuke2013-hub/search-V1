## What was actually done

A public-site crawler checked the existing **107 corporate company records**. It examined each website's top-level navigation for Team/People links and a limited set of on-site candidate paths. It checked up to six pages per eligible company, obeyed robots.txt where retrievable and recorded failed/blocked attempts. A site-wide personnel search or complete crawl of every team biography was **not** performed. The crawler does not infer employee identities or visit LinkedIn.

Total outcomes: 107.

**Interpretation:** a role-term mention is a prompt for individual review, not proof of a specific London office manager. A page without keywords is not proof an office manager does not exist. A blocked, unvisited or missing team page must never be labelled 'no office manager'.

## Website integration

The main SEARCH-V1 navigation contains **Team page audit**. The company detail drawer also shows audit evidence. Site and database share a single embedded snapshot.

## Operations, limitations and follow-up

- Auditing may be rerun with GitHub Actions; changes are recorded with dates, not silently interpreted as a live employment feed.
- No personal LinkedIn profiles are scraped, guessed, generated, or automatically collected.
- For blocked pages, company sites must be checked through a permitted alternative method or left unverified.
- Follow-up should focus on **verified current London office roles** and appropriate public business channels, not mass emailing individual staff.
- Company Team/People pages may change, so confirm employment and purchasing responsibilities before approaching anyone.
- Public repository/site currently contains the above publicly documented professional research. Keep any sensitive CRM, consent and suppression records in protected storage.

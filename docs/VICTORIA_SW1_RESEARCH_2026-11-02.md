## Workflow and safeguards

- Open **Victoria / SW1** in SEARCH-V1. Filter category and evidence tier. The district view combines companies and relevant publicly documented professional roles.
- Follow original employer evidence first. Confirm the role remains current and that facilities, HR, workplace or marketing handles procurement before initiating outreach.
- A company registration is not workplace proof. An announced office lease is not completed occupancy. A listed LinkedIn profile is not an automatically validated live account.
- Recheck the 123 Victoria Street ownership change: Landsec announced a sale in September 2026. Tenant status and property ownership should not be conflated.
- Only automate permitted public pages and APIs. Avoid bulk harvesting platform-restricted LinkedIn pages. Follow applicable UK GDPR and PECR requirements for individual contact data.
- Don't confuse research priority scores with booking probabilities or account purchasing intent.
